About SpaceCo

A space built for focus, community, and momentum.

SpaceCo Workspace is a co-working space in Diamond Mall, Sherouk City — built for anyone who needs a quiet room, a shared table, or a class full of desks, by the hour.

Our Story

Opened November 23, 2024.

SpaceCo was built around a simple idea: not everyone who needs a good place to work needs — or wants — a monthly membership. Some days you need four walls and a whiteboard for an hour. Some days you need a shared table and a coffee corner. SpaceCo was designed to make both of those things easy to walk into, book, and use.

The name is short for "space company," and the tagline — Bring it into focus — is the whole idea in four words: strip away everything that gets in the way of the work, and leave the room to do its job.

What We Believe

Three ideas, everything else follows.

Focus

Every room is designed to remove friction: fast wifi, real air conditioning, quiet by default. Nothing you have to fight to get work done.

Community

The shared space and coffee corner exist on purpose — the people working next to you are half the reason to leave the house at all.

Momentum

No membership required to start. Book an hour, see if it fits, come back when you need to — the room should adapt to you, not the other way around.
